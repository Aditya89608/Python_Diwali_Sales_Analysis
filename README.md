# 📖 Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a **Diwali Sales Dataset** using Python. The objective is to discover customer purchasing behavior, identify sales trends, and generate business insights that can help improve marketing strategies and increase festive season revenue.

The project includes:

- 🧹 Data Cleaning
- 🔍 Data Exploration
- 📊 Data Visualization
- 📈 Customer Analysis
- 💡 Business Insights

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- ✅ Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- ✅ Find the most profitable customer segments.
- ✅ Analyze sales across different states.
- ✅ Discover top-selling product categories.
- ✅ Generate actionable business insights.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

✔️ Married women aged **26–35 years** are among the highest spending customers.

✔️ Customers from selected states contribute the largest share of sales.

✔️ IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals are major buyers.

✔️ Food, Clothing, and Electronics are top-selling categories.
